# Butt Butt

**Created by Dana E. Carlson** · Android · Adults 18+ · In development

> You tell us who you are. You tell us what you're looking for. Butt Butt does the introduction.

Butt Butt is a consent-first dating and social-discovery project built around **emoji Match Recipes**: an expressive way to describe who you are and the connections you want to make.

This is the public project showcase. **The application source is maintained separately in a private repository.** This repository is not an app download or a production launch announcement.

## The idea

Nearby discovery should be straightforward. Describing a compatible connection should be expressive. Consent should never be optional.

Butt Butt places heterosexual adults prominently in its product direction and examples while welcoming all orientations and identities. People describe themselves; the app must not infer identity, appearance, or medical information from photographs.

## What makes a Match Recipe different?

A recipe can describe a one-to-one introduction or a configuration involving several compatible adults.

**Illustrative recipe:** 👩 ↔️ 🧔 🧔

- Choose preferences as **Required**, **Preferred**, or **Don't Care**.
- Use optional emoji modifiers to summarize voluntarily selected appearance preferences. Yellow/default means no skin-tone preference.
- Keep text labels alongside emoji so the meaning is accessible and unambiguous.
- Require reciprocal compatibility and independent acceptance from every participant before a group proposal reveals identities or opens group chat.
- Show approximate distance, never another person's exact coordinates.

Recipes describe compatible introductions; they never imply consent to a meeting or any activity.

## Product commitments

These are requirements for the finished app, not a claim that every feature is currently available.

| Free experience | One Premium subscription |
| --- | --- |
| One-to-one discovery and conversation after mutual consent | Match Recipes involving three or more people |
| Consent, blocking, reporting, unmatching, privacy, and account deletion | Group chat only after everyone independently opts in |
| Universal sexual-health education | Ad-free use and additional recipe convenience |

Free use is planned to include ads outside sensitive surfaces. There is no tier maze and no paid shortcut around consent, safety, or required preferences.

Intent categories include Dating, Relationship, Casual, Friends, and Right Now. Sexual-health education is intended for everyone, without inferring or exposing anyone's medical status.

## Visual identity

Charcoal and near-black surfaces, warm amber accents, off-white text, and a calm safety accent. The name is **Butt Butt**, both words, with its own visual identity.

## Development status

The available prototype includes an Android Compose scaffold, explicit-preference matching domain, consent-state modeling, and the charcoal-and-amber theme. Production accounts, end-to-end safety workflows, backend integration, billing validation, and real-device release testing remain work in progress.
